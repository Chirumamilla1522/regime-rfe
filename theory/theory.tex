\paragraph{Protocol.}
An episodic finite MDP has one of finitely many transition kernels.  Its hidden
kernel is constant within an episode and follows an unknown piecewise-constant
schedule across training episodes.  Training and \(q\) deployment-diagnostic
episodes contain no rewards.  The deployment kernel is fixed and previously
seen.  Only after diagnosis is an arbitrary reward \(r\in\cR\), with pathwise
total reward at most one, revealed.

\paragraph{Segmentation interface.}
On an event of probability \(1-\delta_{\rm seg}\), accepted episodes are pure,
recurrences are matched up to one label permutation, at most \(B\) episodes
at each end of every true block are quarantined, every mode meets its RFE
quota, and all confidence sets cover jointly under adaptive collection.
This is a contract, not a proved detector theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional modular bound]
If segmentation succeeds, every per-mode stationary RFE planner has uniform
error \(\eta\) with failure \(\delta_{\rm rfe}\), and deployment
identification fails with probability at most \(\delta_{\rm id}\), then
\[
\Pr\!\left\{\sup_{r\in\cR}
V_{P^{(Z)}}^\star(r)-V_{P^{(Z)}}^{\widehat\pi_r}(r)\le\eta\right\}
\ge1-\delta_{\rm seg}-M_\star\delta_{\rm rfe}-\delta_{\rm id}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Intersect segmentation, all \(M_\star\) RFE success events, and correct
identification.  A union bound gives the probability; on the intersection the
selected planner is uniformly \(\eta\)-optimal for the deployment kernel.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Binary diagnostic lower bound]
For any adaptive diagnostic strategy \(\sigma\), pair \(m,\ell\), and test,
\[
\Pr_m(\ell)+\Pr_\ell(m)\ge
\tfrac12\exp\{-D_{\rm KL}(\mathbb P_m^\sigma\Vert
\mathbb P_\ell^\sigma)\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The minimum sum of testing errors is \(1-\mathrm{TV}\).  Apply the
Bretagnolle--Huber inequality and
\(1-\sqrt{1-x}\ge x/2\).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Limits.}
With no deployment observations, two deterministic modes with reversed good
actions force worst-case loss at least \(1/2\), even if both models were
learned exactly.  A single-mode stationary RFE lower bound embeds into any one
mode, but multiplying it by the number of modes requires a separate adaptive
direct-sum proof.  Detector-specific segmentation, robust estimated-model
diagnosis, the direct sum, and minimax matching are explicitly unproved
targets.
