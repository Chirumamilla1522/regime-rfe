# Formal model and assumptions ledger

## 1. Episodic kernels and the hidden schedule

Let \(\mathcal S\) and \(\mathcal A\) be finite, \(S=|\mathcal S|\),
\(A=|\mathcal A|\), and \(H\ge 1\). The initial-state distribution
\(\rho\) is known and common to all modes. An episodic transition kernel is
\[
P=(P_h)_{h=1}^H,\qquad
P_h(\cdot\mid s,a)\in\Delta(\mathcal S).
\]
There is an unknown finite family
\(\mathcal P_\star=\{P^{(1)},\ldots,P^{(M_\star)}\}\), where
\(M_\star\le \bar M\) and \(\bar M\) is known. Distinct labels mean distinct
kernels; label permutations have no meaning.

Training lasts \(K\) complete episodes. Before episode \(k\), nature selects a
hidden label \(z_k\in[M_\star]\); that episode is generated entirely by
\(P^{(z_k)}\). Thus switches occur only between episodes. The learner observes
\((s_{k,h},a_{k,h},s_{k,h+1})_{h=1}^H\), but neither \(z_k\), switch boundaries,
nor rewards. Its action at step \(h\) may depend on all prior observations and
its private randomness.

The schedule is piecewise constant. If its maximal constant blocks are
\([b_j,e_j]\), then the minimum dwell condition is
\[
e_j-b_j+1\ge D_{\min}.
\]
Modes may recur in any order. Unless a stochastic schedule is explicitly
introduced, all guarantees are conditional on every deterministic schedule
satisfying the assumptions below; there is no implicit iid mode prior.

## 2. Reward-free training and deployment

After training, nature chooses a deployment label \(Z\in[M_\star]\). It remains
fixed for all diagnostic episodes and subsequent execution. Before seeing any
reward, the learner receives exactly \(q\) fresh diagnostic episodes from
\(P^{(Z)}\). Diagnostic policies may be history-dependent across and within
episodes, but use no reward observations.

After the \(q\)-th diagnostic episode, an arbitrary reward
\[
r=(r_h)_{h=1}^H,\qquad r_h:\mathcal S\times\mathcal A\to[0,1],
\qquad \sup_\tau\sum_{h=1}^H r_h(s_h,a_h)\le 1
\tag{R}
\]
is revealed in full. The pathwise normalization in (R) can be replaced by
\(r_h\in[0,1]\), at the cost of changing every unit loss bound below to \(H\).
There is no interaction after reward revelation. The learner returns a
possibly nonstationary Markov policy
\(\widehat\pi_r=(\widehat\pi_{r,h})_{h=1}^H\).

For kernel \(P\), write
\[
V_P^\pi(r)=
\mathbb E_{\rho,P,\pi}\!\left[\sum_{h=1}^H r_h(s_h,a_h)\right],
\qquad
V_P^\star(r)=\sup_\pi V_P^\pi(r).
\]

### Uniform PAC objective

An algorithm is \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-PAC for a model class if, for every
admissible family, schedule, and deployment mode,
\[
\Pr\!\left\{
  \sup_{r\in\mathcal R_1}
  \left[V_{P^{(Z)}}^\star(r)
       -V_{P^{(Z)}}^{\widehat\pi_r}(r)\right]
  \le\varepsilon
\right\}\ge 1-\delta,
\tag{PAC}
\]
where \(\mathcal R_1\) is the reward class in (R). Probability covers training
and diagnostic trajectories and algorithmic randomness. The supremum is
inside the event: one learned object must support every post-hoc reward
simultaneously. A weaker expected guarantee is stated only when explicitly
labelled.

Deployment is restricted to a mode that appeared during training and for which
the required useful-sample quota was met. If unseen deployment modes are
allowed, DME must return `REJECT/UNSEEN`; no near-optimality guarantee is made
for such a mode without post-reward interaction.

## 3. Diagnostic experiments and information coefficients

A \(q\)-episode diagnostic strategy \(\sigma\) maps observed diagnostic
histories to action distributions. Let
\(\mathbb P_m^\sigma\) be the induced law of the complete \(q\)-episode
transcript under \(P^{(m)}\). This definition includes adaptive strategies.

For distributions \(P,Q\), define affinity
\(\operatorname{Aff}(P,Q)=\int\sqrt{dP\,dQ}\). For a known finite family define
\[
\mathcal B_q(\mathcal P_\star)
=\sup_{\sigma}\min_{m\ne \ell}
   \left[-\log\operatorname{Aff}
     (\mathbb P_m^\sigma,\mathbb P_\ell^\sigma)\right],
\tag{DI}
\]
with \(-\log 0=+\infty\). This is an operational, controlled
distinguishability coefficient: it includes both reachability and the
transition discrepancy generated by the chosen controls. It need not equal
\(q\) times a one-episode coefficient because cross-episode adaptation can
matter.

For lower bounds, for a fixed strategy \(\sigma\), use
\[
\mathcal K_q^\sigma(m,\ell)
=D_{\rm KL}(\mathbb P_m^\sigma\Vert\mathbb P_\ell^\sigma).
\tag{KL}
\]
The chain rule expresses this as expected accumulated one-step transition KL;
state-actions never reached by \(\sigma\) contribute zero.

With estimated models, the robust design objective is
\[
\widehat\sigma\in\arg\max_\sigma\;
\min_{m\ne\ell}\inf_{P\in\mathcal C_m,Q\in\mathcal C_\ell}
-\log\operatorname{Aff}(\mathbb P_P^\sigma,\mathbb P_Q^\sigma).
\tag{RDI}
\]
If the inner minimum is zero, the library is not certifiably diagnosable at the
current accuracy and the algorithm must abstain or collect more reward-free
data; it must not silently assert a label.

## 4. Assumptions ledger

### A1. Probe reachability

There is a finite probe set
\(\mathcal X\subseteq[H]\times\mathcal S\times\mathcal A\). For each
\(x=(h,s,a)\in\mathcal X\), a reward-free policy reaches \((s,a)\) at step \(h\)
with probability at least \(\mu_{\rm probe}>0\), uniformly over all modes.

**Used by:** calibrated switch detection and matching.

**Why some form is necessary:** if two modes differ only at a state-action-step
triple that no policy can reach from \(\rho\), their entire transcript laws are
identical and they cannot be distinguished. Unreachable differences also
cannot affect value, so the right assumption may instead quotient kernels by
behavioral equivalence; requiring entrywise separation everywhere would be
unnecessarily strong.

### A2. Controlled separation

For every \(m\ne\ell\), the diagnostic transcript laws are separated:
\(\mathcal B_q(\mathcal P_\star)>0\), and the desired error level requires a
quantitative margin, e.g.
\[
\mathcal B_q(\mathcal P_\star)
\ge \log\!\frac{\bar M-1}{2\delta_{\rm id}}.
\]
For training-time detection one additionally needs a probe-local discrepancy,
for example some \(x\in\mathcal X\) with
\(\|P_h^{(m)}(\cdot|s,a)-P_h^{(\ell)}(\cdot|s,a)\|_1\ge\Delta_{\rm det}\).

**Used by:** switch detection, recurrence matching, deployment diagnosis.

**Counterexample:** two labels with identical transition kernels induce
identical reward-free observations. No transition-only procedure can recover
their semantic labels. More subtly, positive entrywise separation at
unreachable triples gives zero controlled information.

### A3. Useful occupancy / recurrence

For each mode \(m\), after removing boundary quarantine and diagnostic probes,
the schedule and exploration mechanism provide at least
\(N_{\rm RFE}^{(m)}(\varepsilon_{\rm mdl},\delta_{\rm rfe})\) correctly assigned
episodes satisfying the input contract of the chosen stationary RFE routine.
Equivalently, one can require at least \(J_m\) sufficiently long occurrences
and prove that DME supplies this quota.

**Used by:** reward-uniform model learning for every deployment-eligible mode.

**Counterexample:** a mode appearing for one episode cannot in general be
learned to arbitrary accuracy, even if all other modes recur infinitely often.
A known upper bound \(\bar M\) does not imply adequate occupancy.

### A4. Dwell time

\[
D_{\min}\ge
L_{\rm detect}+2B_{\rm quarantine}+L_{\rm match}+L_{\rm useful},
\]
where each term is a declared episode budget.  Section 6 fixes the accounting
convention: boundary quarantine is charged once to block ends, and accepted
probe, matching, and useful-RFE episode sets must be declared disjoint unless
the relevant module theorem explicitly proves valid reuse.

**Used by:** obtaining uncontaminated samples after a hidden switch.

**Counterexample:** if modes alternate every episode while detection needs two
samples from the same mode, no window-based detector can form a pure
post-change window. A stronger latent-MDP method might handle rapid switching,
but that is a different model and proof.

### A5. Reward normalization

Rewards satisfy (R).

**Used by:** converting mode-identification error into at most one unit of
policy loss and making \(\varepsilon\) scale-independent.

**Counterexample:** multiplying all rewards by \(c\) multiplies every policy
gap by \(c\); no scale-free additive PAC statement is possible without a
bound.

### A6. Fixed, previously seen deployment mode

The deployment kernel is one training mode and remains fixed through all
\(q\) diagnostics and execution.

**Used by:** pooling diagnostic evidence and selecting a stored model.

**Counterexample:** if the mode changes after diagnosis, the diagnostic label
need not describe execution. If the deployment mode is unseen, a library-only
planner has no uniformly accurate transition model for it. Rejection is
possible under separation; planning is not.

### A7. Transition-only regimes

Modes differ only through \(P^{(m)}\). Rewards are absent in training and
diagnosis and are supplied only after diagnosis.

**Used by:** transition-residual detection and reward-uniform reuse.

**Counterexample:** “goal drift” that changes only an unobserved reward leaves
the reward-free transcript law unchanged. Such regimes cannot be detected by
transition-only diagnostics. If rewards are mode-specific, the problem must
specify whether the revealed reward itself identifies the mode.

### A8. Confidence validity under adaptive collection

Every confidence set \(\mathcal C_m\) is time-uniform or constructed at declared
stopping times with a valid adaptive-data argument.

**Used by:** matching, robust diagnostics, and all union bounds.

**Counterexample:** applying fixed-sample multinomial intervals after a
data-dependent alarm can undercover; then a false match can contaminate all
future recurrence data.

## 5. Behavioral rather than syntactic modes

The cleanest theory defines two kernels as equivalent when they induce the same
trajectory law under every policy. Only equivalence classes are identifiable.
For finite fully observed MDPs, differences on reachable state-action-step
triples generate a distinguishing policy; differences confined to unreachable
triples do not. DME should count behavioral classes, not arbitrary duplicated
labels.

## 6. Precise segmentation contract

Let \(\mathcal B=\{[b_j,e_j]\}_{j=1}^J\) be the maximal true constant
blocks.  A segmentation module receives the training transcript and returns

* an accepted set \(U\subseteq[K]\);
* a quarantine set \(Q=[K]\setminus U\);
* an assignment \(c:U\to[\widehat M]\); and
* confidence sets \((\mathcal C_i)_{i=1}^{\widehat M}\).

For budgets \(B\ge0\), quotas \(n_1,\ldots,n_{M_\star}\), and error
\(\delta_{\rm seg}\), the output satisfies
\(\operatorname{SEG}(B,\mathbf n,\delta_{\rm seg})\) when, with probability at
least \(1-\delta_{\rm seg}\), there is a bijection
\(\phi:[\widehat M]\to[M_\star]\) such that simultaneously:

1. **Purity:** \(c(k)=i\) implies \(z_k=\phi(i)\).  No accepted episode is
   assigned across a true boundary.
2. **Boundary localization:** for every block \([b_j,e_j]\),
   \[
   Q\cap[b_j,e_j]\subseteq
   [b_j,b_j+B-1]\cup[e_j-B+1,e_j],
   \]
   after intersecting intervals with \([b_j,e_j]\).  Thus at most \(2B\)
   episodes per block are discarded; this condition does not require an alarm
   at a particular timestamp.
3. **No splitting or merging:** all accepted episodes having true label \(m\)
   receive the unique label \(\phi^{-1}(m)\), including nonconsecutive
   recurrences.  Every mode with \(n_m>0\) is represented.
4. **Quota and RFE admissibility:** \(U_m=\{k\in U:z_k=m\}\) has
   \(|U_m|\ge n_m\), and the ordered trajectories in \(U_m\), together with
   the policies that generated them, satisfy the declared input contract of
   the stationary RFE routine.  In particular, purity alone does not imply
   iid sampling.
5. **Confidence validity:** \(P^{(m)}\in\mathcal C_{\phi^{-1}(m)}\) for every
   represented mode, jointly and despite adaptive alarms and assignments.

This is an interface, not a proved property of a particular detector.  A
detector-specific theorem must derive \(B\), the quotas, and its dwell-time
condition from \((\mu_{\rm probe},\Delta_{\rm det},S,A,H,\delta_{\rm seg})\).
The boundary convention is deliberately non-overlapping: quarantine is
charged to the two ends of each true block, while accepted interior episodes
are charged once to the corresponding mode's RFE quota.

## 7. Stationary RFE module contract

For a class \(\mathfrak P\), an
\(\operatorname{RFE}(\eta,\delta_{\rm rfe},N)\) module maps \(N\) admissibly
collected pure episodes from one unknown \(P\in\mathfrak P\) to a planner
\(\Pi\) such that
\[
\Pr\!\left\{\sup_{r\in\mathcal R_1}
[V_P^\star(r)-V_P^{\Pi(r)}(r)]\le\eta\right\}
\ge1-\delta_{\rm rfe}.
\]
Any invocation must state whether \(\mathfrak P\) is time-homogeneous or
time-inhomogeneous and must use the reward normalization of its cited result;
rescaling by \(H\) changes the displayed \(\varepsilon\)-dependence.
